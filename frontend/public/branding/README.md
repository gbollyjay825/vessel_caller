# NPA logo

`npa-logo.png` is the original Nigerian Ports Authority logo downloaded from its official website:

https://nigerianports.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/npa1.png

Source page: https://nigerianports.gov.ng/

Retrieved on 12 October 2026. The source image is unchanged.

The favicon and app icons use the coat of arms from the left 128 × 136 pixels
of this source, with centered padding and a white background for contrast on
light and dark browser tabs. They are cropped and resized copies of the
official artwork; the full logo above remains unchanged.

- `../favicon.svg`: self-contained browser icon, with the cropped source embedded.
- `npa-favicon.ico`: browser fallback at 16, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 pixels.
- `npa-favicon-32.png`: 32-pixel browser icon.
- `npa-apple-touch-icon.png`: 180-pixel home screen icon.
- `npa-icon-192.png` and `npa-icon-512.png`: installed app icons.
